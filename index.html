For iPhone, iPad, Mac & Android · Windows coming soon · Works fully offline

Photo to pattern

Any photo or drawing becomes a stitchable chart — live preview, adjustable stitch count, one-tap background removal, and up to 256 colors matched to real DMC and Anchor threads.

Free progress tracking

Add a pattern to Progress and tap stitches as you finish them — backstitches and French knots too. See progress per color and stitch type, park threads with parking marks, and bring in OXS, .xsd and .pat charts.

Layers & pro tools

Up to 10 layers, a trace image, shapes, text, fill, eyedropper and Magic Wand, plus selections you can move, copy, mirror or rotate. On iPad, add Apple Pencil and customizable keyboard shortcuts.

Every stitch type

Full, half, quarter, three-quarter and petite stitches, backstitch lines, French knots, and beads — the complete kit.

Smooth at 1000 × 1000

Charts up to 1000 × 1000 stitches and 256 colors — panning, zooming and stitching never lag. Switch between cross, symbol and flat views any time.

One-tap confetti cleanup

The confetti map marks stray single stitches. Paint over an area and swap them for the surrounding color in one tap.

Thread-true palettes

Blend up to 4 threads, set strand counts per color, and give every color its own unique chart symbol.

Bring your patterns

Import OXS, Pattern Maker (.xsd) and PCStitch (.pat) files and keep working right where you left off.

Print-ready PDF

Export vector charts with a symbol chart, thread list and floss estimates — set the paper size, center lines and symbol size, and preview every page for free before you unlock.

Free to design. Free to track.

Converting, designing, editing and progress tracking are free, with up to 3 saved projects and 3 tracked patterns. To export, save or track more, and apply Declutter, unlock with a yearly subscription (7 days free for new subscribers) or a one-time purchase — the same features either way, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. If a subscription ends, only those features lock again; your projects are never deleted.
